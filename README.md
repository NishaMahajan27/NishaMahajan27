# 💫 About Me:

<br>

Hi, I'm **Nisha Mahajan** from Nashik, Maharashtra. I hold a **Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)** degree from **Kavayitri Bahinabai Chaudhari North Maharashtra University, Jalgaon**, and have **7 months of hands-on experience as a DevOps Intern**.

My interests lie in **Cloud Computing, DevOps, Infrastructure Automation, and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)**. I have practical experience working with Linux systems, cloud platforms, CI/CD pipelines, containerization, and monitoring solutions.

My technical expertise includes AWS services such as **EC2, VPC, IAM, S3, RDS, Route 53, Lambda, CloudWatch, EFS, and CloudFront**, along with DevOps tools including **Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, Jenkins, Git, GitHub, and Datadog**.

I enjoy designing automated workflows, improving deployment efficiency, and building reliable cloud-native solutions. I am continuously expanding my knowledge of modern DevOps practices and cloud technologies while focusing on scalability, automation, and operational excellence.
